Happier is the same account and the same sessions everywhere. What changes is
the surface you reach them through, and how much of the machine that surface
can see.

## Where Happier runs

- [App](/apps/app) — desktop, web and mobile. The one you will spend your time in.
- [Desktop control panel](/apps/control-panel) — the desktop app doing the jobs that need a real machine: relay, daemon and secure-access setup.
- [Windows](/apps/windows) — the behaviour and troubleshooting that only applies there.
- [Custom mobile build](/apps/custom-mobile-build) — building your own app against a self-hosted server, without going through the stores.

## Getting a machine connected

- [Setup wizard](/apps/setup-wizard) — guided relay selection, login, machine setup and secure access.
- [Relay access](/apps/relay-access) — making a self-hosted relay reachable from your other devices, over Tailscale, Cloudflare, the LAN, or not at all.
- [Remote hosts (SSH)](/apps/remote-hosts) — saved SSH targets the setup flows reuse.

## What every surface shares

Whichever one you use, the control plane underneath is the same: the same
account and server selection, the same catalog of actions an agent or a person
can invoke, the same per-surface settings for those actions, and the same
approval flow when an action is gated.

That is why an approval raised by a session on your laptop can be answered from
your phone, and why voice and an external MCP host can drive the same actions
the app exposes.
