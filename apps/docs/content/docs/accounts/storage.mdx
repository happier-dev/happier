**Settings → Storage** shows what your Account keeps on the Home you are signed
in to, and what you can clear.

> **Availability:** Storage is under development for 0.3. This page does not
> claim stable or preview availability.

## What the page shows

The page opens with one sentence: how much your Account stores and which kind
holds the most. Below it:

- **Your Account** is always shown. Its bar splits the total into Artifacts,
  Discussion attachments, Pets, plugin data and Artifacts saved by plugins. The
  Home does not measure profile pictures, avatars or Team logos yet. When you
  have any, the total reads "stored or more" and those kinds say their size is
  not recorded.
- **Artifact limit** appears only when the operator sets one. Artifacts and
  their old versions count toward it. Pets and plugin data have their own
  limits. Near the limit, at 90% or more, the figure turns amber. Over the
  limit, the page shows the same notice as the Artifacts page, with an action
  that lists your biggest Artifacts.
- **What's stored** lists each kind with its size. Pressing Artifacts lists them
  below. Pets and plugin data open the page where they live.
- **Biggest items** lists your own Artifacts, largest first, with each one's
  stored size: the current version, its retained old versions and their files.
  Artifacts shared with you count toward their owner and are not listed. On a
  wide window, **Map** shows the same items as a treemap. An Artifact this device
  cannot decrypt keeps its size and date and says its name is not readable here.

Every size comes from the Home. The app does not add sizes up or estimate them.

## Clear space

Each item's **…** menu offers **Open**, **Show old versions** and **Delete…**.
Delete asks for confirmation first and is offered only when you may delete that
Artifact. After a delete, the totals update from the Home.

**Ask an agent** starts an ordinary Session with a prompt about your storage.
The agent reads the same numbers and proposes what to clear. Nothing is deleted
without your approval.

## Open Storage from elsewhere

- On the **Artifacts** page, press the stored total, or the meter when a limit
  is set. Storage opens with Artifacts chosen. Back returns to Artifacts.
- When saving fails because the Account is full, the notice in the Artifacts
  page and in the editor offers **See what's using space**. A single document
  that is too large to save has no link, because its own size is the limit.
