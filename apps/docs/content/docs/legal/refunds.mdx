## Web and desktop purchases

Request a full refund within 14 days of your first payment or a one-time Supporter Pack purchase. If you forgot to cancel a monthly subscription, request a refund within 14 days of that renewal charge.

Use the link on your receipt or write to [hello@happier.dev](mailto:hello@happier.dev) with the email on your receipt. Polar, the merchant of record, refunds the original payment method.

## App Store and Google Play purchases

Request your refund from [Apple](https://reportaproblem.apple.com/) or [Google Play](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637). Each store applies its own refund policy.

## What happens after a refund

When a refund ends Lite or Plus, the plan ends immediately and the [90-day history grace period](/legal/terms-of-sale#after-a-history-plan-ends) starts then. After the grace period, Free limits apply and existing pins stay pinned.

If the refund leaves you with no qualifying purchase, your Supporter Pack ends. Your in-app perks and public listings are removed, and your supporter number no longer appears. Your number stays reserved for you if you get the pack again.

This policy is in addition to any mandatory consumer rights that apply to you.
