## What you can buy

- **Happier Lite** costs $2.99 per month. It includes 90 days of session history, 14 days of subagent transcripts, 100 pins and the Supporter Pack on Happier Cloud.
- **Happier Plus** costs $6.99 per month. It includes one year of session history, 30 days of subagent transcripts, 500 pins and the Supporter Pack on Happier Cloud.
- **The Supporter Pack** is a digital product for use with any Happier server. The pack is delivered automatically as soon as payment is confirmed. It includes a supporter number, an in-app pass and avatar badge, and an optional listing on the [supporters wall](https://happier.dev/supporters), in Credits and in the README. The seller shows the final price, currency and applicable taxes before you pay. Your self-hosted server follows its own storage policy. See [Plans & Supporter](https://happier.dev/plans) for Happier Cloud limits.

## Renewal and cancellation

Monthly subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel. To stop the next renewal, use the customer portal linked from your web or desktop receipt, Subscriptions in your Apple account, or Subscriptions in Google Play. You keep your paid plan until the end of the period. The Supporter Pack stays with you after cancellation.

## After a history plan ends

Your account keeps its paid history limits for 90 days after the plan ends. Then Free limits apply. Existing pins stay pinned, including pins above the Free limit. The app shows eligible sessions seven days before their messages leave Happier Cloud. These sessions stay in your list under External.

See the [refund policy](/legal/refunds) for what changes when a payment is refunded.

## Changes to plans and limits

We announce changes to limits in the app at least 30 days ahead. If we reduce the limits you paid for during a paid period, you can cancel and ask for a prorated refund for the unused part of that period. Price changes take effect at renewal after notice under the seller's terms.

We may change or discontinue Happier Cloud or a paid plan.
