---
title: Protocol
description: The event contract between clients, the relay and the machines running your agents.
---

Everything Happier does across devices rides on one contract: a versioned event
protocol shared by the clients, the relay and the CLI daemon.

Two properties of it are worth knowing even if you never read the schemas:

- **The socket carries the data, both ways.** Message bodies arrive over the
  WebSocket rather than being fetched over HTTP afterwards, and clients write
  back over the same connection.
- **Writes are optimistically concurrent.** Versioned fields carry an
  `expectedVersion`, and a stale write is rejected with the current version
  attached rather than overwriting. Conflict resolution belongs to the client,
  which is what lets two devices work on one session without locking it.

[How Happier fits together](/self-hosting/how-it-works) covers what that means in practice,
including why it matters when you put a reverse proxy in front of a self-hosted
relay.
