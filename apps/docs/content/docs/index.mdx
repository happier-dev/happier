Happier runs coding agents — Claude Code, Codex, OpenCode, Cursor, Gemini and a
dozen more — on computers you control, and lets you watch, steer, approve and
reply to them from your phone, a browser, or another machine. The work happens
on your hardware, in your repository, with your agent's own credentials.

Sessions sync end-to-end encrypted. On a hosted relay, and on a self-hosted
server at its defaults, the server holds ciphertext and routing metadata and
cannot read your conversations. A self-hosted operator can choose a plaintext
storage policy instead — [Security](/security) covers exactly what changes.

## If something is not working

[Check your setup](/getting-started/check-your-setup) explains `happier status`
and `happier doctor repair`, which between them diagnose and fix most of what
goes wrong on a machine.
