---
title: Notifications
description: Send host-mediated notifications and register declared delivery channels.
---

Invoked handlers send notifications through `context.services.notifications`. Activation registers executable delivery channels through `api.notifications.registerChannel(localId, sender)` for matching manifest-declared ids.

Use the service to send or inspect the supported notification categories, channels, and preferences exposed by the installed SDK version. Use `context.ui.notify(...)` instead when the intent belongs to the current present-user invocation rather than the notification system.

## Rules

- Declare categories and channels in the cold manifest.
- Respect user preferences and host availability results.
- Do not call platform notification APIs directly from an Agent leaf.
- Keep titles, bodies, and data free of secrets and raw Provider tokens.
