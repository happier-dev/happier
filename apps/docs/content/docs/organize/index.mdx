---
title: Staying on top of work
description: Keeping a long session list navigable, knowing what still needs you, and finding your way back to earlier work.
---

Running one session is easy. Running twenty is a filing problem, and this is
where Happier helps with it.

- [Organizing sessions](/organize/session-organization) — folders, tags, pins and bulk actions, all synced across your devices.
- [Read and unread state](/organize/session-read-state) — what marks a session as needing you, and how to keep one in Needs attention after you have read it.
- [Navigating a transcript](/organize/transcript-navigation) — jumping between turns, pinning messages, and sending a selection into another session.
- [Local memory search](/organize/local-memory-search) — finding earlier work by meaning rather than by remembering the exact words.
- [Tool timeline](/organize/tool-timeline) — how tool calls are normalized and displayed.
