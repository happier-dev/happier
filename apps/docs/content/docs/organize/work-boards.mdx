---
title: Work Boards
description: Keep sessions, workflow runs, workflows and machines together in a live board, arranged by position or status.
---

Keep the work you check together in one place. A Work Board combines sessions,
workflow runs, workflow definitions and machines as cards, with status taken from
the underlying item. Open a card to return to that work.

## Availability

Work Boards are **0.3 development behavior**, not a stable-release feature.
Use a current 0.3 client and Home with the current Artifacts implementation.
Workflow content additionally depends on Workflows being available on that Home.

The **Boards** destination described here is separate from a session's
[Session Board and Companion](/sessions/session-board), which hosts widgets for
one session.

## Create and fill a board

1. Open **Boards** in the app navigation and choose **New board**.
2. Open **Board settings** from the header's ⋯ menu and give the board a name.
3. Choose what appears automatically: **Needs you**, **Running now**,
   **My machines**, or **Sessions**. The Sessions filter uses the same choices as
   the Sessions list; its default selects both sessions and workflow runs,
   with **Started by** set to you.
4. Use **Add to board** to search for sessions, workflow runs, workflows or
   machines and add individual items. Items added this way appear under
   **Added by hand** in Board settings.

In current 0.3 development source, **Add to board → Widgets** opens the shared
widget Gallery. Choose a built-in, installed or reusable widget; fill missing
inputs in **Set up** before adding. You can add another copy with different
inputs. **Edit inputs** changes only that copy, while editing a reusable
definition affects its references. Widgets use the same Board document and
save operations as work cards, with widths of one or two card columns.

Sources can be combined. The same item appears once even if it matches a source
and was also added by hand. Removing a hand-picked item does not remove it while
an automatic source still includes it. Change that source or its filter if you
want the item to leave the board.

## Arrange and open work

**Canvas** lets you drag cards into position. **Snap to grid** is controlled in
Board settings. On the web, hold Shift during a drag to snap that move even when
snapping is off. A focused Canvas card can also be moved with arrow keys; native
accessibility actions offer directional moves.

**By status** groups work cards into **Needs you · Working · Finished · Idle ·
Offline**. Switching layouts keeps the saved Canvas positions. Phones show the
stacked By status view and retain those positions for wider devices. See the
[work glossary](/organize/work-glossary#status-buckets-and-state-words) for the
difference between a group and the word on a card.

Widgets appear first in By status, in Board order. Their saved widths remain
available for wider layouts even when the phone displays one column. The
widget composition remains under development and loaded-platform validation.

Cards show summaries: session awareness; a workflow run's state, attention and
available step progress; a workflow's triggers, last run and needs-you count;
or machine presence and counts from loaded sessions. Scheduled next-run times
currently show **Not loaded yet** on Board cards rather than a calculated time.
Missing summary data is not a zero count or proof that no run is scheduled.
Open the workflow or run for its own detail surface.

A Needs you session or run card opens its item in **Inbox** when that destination
is available; otherwise it opens the work itself. Answer permissions or review
requests there. Board cards do not approve work.

## Keep a board near your sessions

Turn on **Show in the Sessions list** in Board settings to show that board above the
Sessions list, including its needs-you count. Pinning is off for a new board.
The Sessions list's pinned-board visibility preference also controls whether
those rows are shown.

A board is saved as one Account document on the Home where you created it.
Its item references retain their own Home identity. Putting a card on a board
does not grant access to that item; sharing the board also does not share its
sessions or workflows. See [Sharing](/accounts/session-sharing).

## When a card or save is unavailable

**Unavailable** means this device cannot currently serve that card's Home or
content; it is not proof that the work was deleted or finished. A retained item
from an unavailable Home keeps its saved position. Workflow and run projections
are currently scoped to the active Account/Home, so even a connected second
Home can be unavailable in this view. Return to the appropriate Home to inspect
the work.

A failed read offers retry rather than treating the board collection as empty.
If a save is refused, the board returns to its last acknowledged state and shows
the save failure. Use **Try again** after correcting the cause, or **Dismiss** to
clear the notice. A missing board cannot be repaired by retrying its edit.
If the board exceeds Board storage, remove some items before trying again;
changing Account settings does not address that storage failure.
Deleting a board removes its arrangement, not the sessions, runs, workflows or
machines it references.
