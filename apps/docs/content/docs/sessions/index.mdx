This is the loop you spend most of your time in. A session is one conversation
with one agent, running on one of your machines — and everything here is about
driving it well from wherever you happen to be.

## The basics

- [Starting a session](/sessions/start-a-session) — choosing the machine, folder, agent and model.
- [New-session shortcuts](/sessions/new-session-shortcuts) — getting back into a familiar setup without walking the picker again.
- [Permissions](/sessions/permissions) — what the agent may do without asking. Worth reading before you leave one running unattended.
- [Queueing and delivery](/sessions/pending-queue) — sending while the agent is busy, and what happens to a message that cannot be delivered yet.
- [Steering](/sessions/steering) — correcting a turn already in flight instead of interrupting it.
- [Approvals and the inbox](/sessions/inbox-and-approvals) — where requests for permission surface.
