# Waiting and watchers (moved from .claude/CLAUDE.md "Claude usage economy")

- **Waiting:** follow root **Efficient execution and uncertainty resolution** and the discovered Happier run contract in `.claude/CLAUDE.md`
  for notification/interruptible-wait priority; wait on each delegated run's own completion signal (`execution_run_wait` / discovered `execution.run.wait` and run status), or a background job's own notification. A report-file watcher is only a backup; use one bounded watcher for facts with no event path.
  Re-arm it every turn while that wait remains active, keep a persistent seen set, match addressed posts anywhere in the heading (excluding your own and your lanes' board posts), and diff before rebaselining; never detach it.
  Use the canonical `scripts/board/watchBoard.mjs` (run_in_background): filter to addressed headings first, never truncate that set, never bulk-rebaseline; it wakes on ASK/URGENT/RULING addressed to you or your lanes and, as a backup, on any final verdict word in a lane report's first line, including legacy verdicts, with a heartbeat. Every wake runs `execution_run_list status=failed` because dead runs may write nothing (owner 2026-11-02).
  On every wake, also confirm the watcher process is alive and compare each running lane's report/evidence with the previous wake; if progress has not advanced recently, inspect that run's status and pending operation before intervening. Resume usage-limit-parked runs immediately through the existing continuation/retry contract; `recovering_usage_limit` with a retry hours away is parked, not running. If a message hit a finished run, resume its continuation and resend the message there (UI refinements, owner-approved 2026-11-04). When a run delivers a terminal completion, compare it with the report's first line; a missing or nonterminal verdict needs diagnosis and, if work remains, continuation on the existing handle, never an assumption that the lane finished (UI refinements, 2026-11-06: ended runs hid unfinished lanes overnight). Before ending any turn, confirm that a watcher carrying your own `--name` is alive, not just that some watcher exists (Coordination, 11-06/07: about 16.5 h with no own watcher while other leads' watchers ran; ORCH-TOP: reload #82's failure unseen for about 2.5 h).
  Wake immediately on ASK/URGENT/RULING/HOLD, and on any RESULT addressed to your program or lanes by name: a named "producer committed" RESULT read as FYI once idled a lane about 3 h (11-02). Batch RESULTs where you are only cc'd or addressed via ALL into the next wake or heartbeat (Coordination + Systemic, 2026-11-04). A local reload HOLD or LIFTED wakes only consumers of the named stack, fixture or borrowed service; other stacks' local notices are irrelevant. Named producer RESULTs and qualifying ALL-leads RULING/URGENT/HOLD still wake at once (PERF ORCH and Systemic, 2026-11-06: unrelated reloads repeatedly woke their watchers).
  A Claude lane waiting on its own background job blocks on that job instead of ending its turn with "waiting…"; it ends its turn only at a material transition or when parked, and every parked/queued item has an owner-visible resume trigger.
  All lanes stop their background jobs before their final message, so finished lanes leave no waiting wakes.

Lane reports use `IN PROGRESS — <current work>` on the first line until final handoff, then exactly one verdict: `DONE`, `BLOCKED`, or `IMPLEMENTED_NOT_VERIFIED`. Put evidence or the missing prerequisite below it; interim updates never use a final verdict.
